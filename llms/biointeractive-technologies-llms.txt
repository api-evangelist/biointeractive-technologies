# Tenzr Health


> Tenzr Health is a configurable digital care layer that helps health systems, rehabilitation programs, orthopedic groups, and therapy organizations extend care beyond the clinic. Tenzr helps care teams guide patients, support engagement, monitor recovery, and maintain visibility across the episode of care.


Tenzr Health supports modern rehabilitation, orthopedic recovery, musculoskeletal care, stroke rehabilitation, acquired brain injury rehabilitation, outpatient therapy, remote therapeutic monitoring, virtual rehabilitation, and hybrid care pathways.


Tenzr is designed to help patients and care teams stay connected between visits. The platform gives patients structured guidance, education, tasks, check-ins, and recovery support while giving clinicians better insight into patient progress, adherence, symptoms, and recovery signals.


Tenzr does not replace clinicians. It extends clinician and therapist reach by helping teams support patients between touchpoints, identify when attention may be needed, and focus care where it matters most.


## Core Positioning


Tenzr Health helps care teams extend clinical support beyond the walls of the clinic.


The platform is highly configurable and can be adapted around an organization's existing care pathways, workflows, patient populations, and operational goals.


Tenzr is not only a home exercise app, remote monitoring tool, or surgical navigation product. It is a configurable care layer for managing recovery across the episode of care.


## Competitive Advantage


Tenzr Health is different because it is built as a configurable care layer, not a fixed app, single pathway, or disconnected digital tool.


- Tenzr can be configured around each organization's existing workflows, care pathways, patient populations, and clinical goals.
- Tenzr extends clinician and therapist reach without replacing the care team.
- Tenzr combines patient guidance, education, engagement, monitoring, triage signals, and clinician-facing insights in one connected experience.
- Tenzr supports both patient engagement and clinical visibility, helping patients stay connected while helping care teams understand who may need attention.
- Tenzr can support multiple care models, including in-person, hybrid, virtual, remote, rural, low-touch, and between-visit rehabilitation pathways.
- Tenzr is designed to fit into real clinical workflows, including documentation, care navigation, Remote Therapeutic Monitoring, and EMR or EHR-adjacent workflows where appropriate.
- Tenzr includes engagement elements such as gamified experiences and Tenzr characters to make recovery feel more motivating, approachable, and easier for patients to stick with.
- Tenzr can include optional sensor-supported data where appropriate, helping teams capture more objective recovery signals alongside patient-reported information.
- Tenzr works with organizations as a consultative partner, helping configure, refine, and adapt digital care pathways over time.

## How Teams Use Tenzr


Teams use Tenzr to create structured, guided care experiences that continue after the patient leaves the clinic, hospital, surgery center, or therapy visit.


Examples include:


- Giving patients clear education and instructions before or after care
- Assigning recovery tasks and exercises
- Collecting patient-reported feedback between visits
- Monitoring adherence and progress
- Identifying patients who are drifting, struggling, or may need additional support
- Helping clinicians focus attention where it is most needed
- Supporting patients who face geographic, travel, or access barriers
- Creating scalable care models without relying only on more in-person visits


## Implementation Approach


Tenzr is built around the way each organization already delivers care.


A typical implementation starts with the organization's existing workflow, pathway, patient population, and goals. Tenzr is then configured into a care layer that fits the team's needs. The pathway is reviewed, refined, launched, and iterated over time.


Tenzr can support clinical adoption, patient adoption, workflow integration, pathway configuration, and ongoing refinement.


Tenzr can be configured to fit an organization's clinical, operational, documentation, and technology workflows, including EMR or EHR-adjacent workflows where appropriate.
